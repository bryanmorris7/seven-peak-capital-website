Investment Thesis

Why Private Credit. Why Now.

Private credit has become one of the most compelling risk-adjusted opportunities in the current environment. As traditional bank lending has tightened, the gap between capital demand and institutional supply has created a structural lending premium — which we capture through disciplined origination.

Income Fund II deploys capital into short-duration, senior-secured first-lien loans where principal risk is mitigated by conservative loan-to-value ratios. This structure allows us to generate consistent, contractual income rather than relying on asset appreciation or favorable exit conditions.

The result: consistent monthly income, capital protected by real estate collateral, and returns that are largely uncorrelated to public market volatility.

Investor FAQ

Common Questions

Income Fund II is open exclusively to accredited investors as defined under Rule 501 of Regulation D. An accredited investor generally includes individuals with annual income exceeding $200,000 (or $300,000 combined with a spouse) in each of the two most recent years, or individuals with a net worth exceeding $1 million excluding primary residence. Accreditation is verified through Parallel Markets prior to investment.
The minimum investment in Income Fund II is $50,000, with a 10% annualized preferred return and a 50/50 profit split above the preferred. The fund is designed to provide monthly cash distributions to your designated bank account via ACH or wire transfer. Distribution timing and amounts are subject to fund performance and the terms outlined in the offering documents.
Income Fund II is structured as a Wyoming Limited Liability Company and is offered under Rule 506(c) of Regulation D, promulgated by the SEC. Under 506(c), the fund may publicly advertise its offering but must verify that all investors are accredited. Seven Peak Capital LLC serves as the manager of the fund. Full structural details, rights, and obligations are set forth in the Operating Agreement and Private Placement Memorandum.
Income Fund II deploys capital into short-term, senior-secured first-lien loans to vetted residential real estate operators — single-family and small multifamily projects with typical 6–9 month timelines. All positions are underwritten with conservative loan-to-value ratios, typically at or below 75%. This collateral cushion is designed to protect investor principal in the event of borrower default. All underwriting, documentation, and risk management practices are described in detail in the offering documents.
Income Fund II deploys capital through Seven Peak's partnership with DeMok Capital, an investor-facing private real estate credit platform focused on disciplined underwriting and transparent investor reporting. DeMok originates and services senior-secured, first-lien loans on residential investment real estate. As of July 2026, the DeMok platform reports 53 loans funded, $18M+ originated, 36 loans fully repaid, and zero principal losses.
Full liquidity, lock-up, and redemption terms for Income Fund II will be set forth in the fund's Operating Agreement and Private Placement Memorandum, released at launch. Investors should review the liquidity provisions in the offering documents before making an investment decision.
