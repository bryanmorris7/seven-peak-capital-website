Underwriting

 

How to read a deal in 10 minutes.

Skip the pitch deck. Go to four numbers.

Sponsors love a 60-page deck. Hero shots, market data, charts that point up and to the right. Most of it is decoration.

If you only have 10 minutes, look at four lines.

01  ·  DSCR

Debt Service Coverage Ratio

How much income covers the debt. Look for 1.25x or higher in year one. Below 1.0 means the property doesn't pay its own mortgage.

 

02  ·  GOING-IN CAP

Yield at acquisition

NOI divided by purchase price. If it's lower than the interest rate, you're paying for hope, not income.

 

03  ·  EXIT CAP

Sale assumption

The cap rate they assume at sale. It should be higher than the going-in cap — at least 50–75 bps. If a sponsor models cap compression to make the deal work, the deal doesn't work.

 

04  ·  DEBT STRUCTURE

How they're financed

Fixed or floating. Term length. Interest-only period. Floating-rate bridge debt with a short maturity is what blows up over-leveraged syndications when rates move against the deal. Don't be the lesson.

A pitch deck is a sales tool.
The numbers are the product.
