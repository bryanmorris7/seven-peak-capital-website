Cash Flow First

 

The only number that matters.

Net worth is a vanity metric.

It moves on paper. It looks great in a financial planning app. It doesn't pay your mortgage when you stop working.

There's only one number that actually changes your life:

Monthly cash flow that arrives
whether you work or not.

That's the number that lets you take a year off. Quit the job that's eating you alive. Fund the next chapter.

It's the number every wealthy family I know tracks. Not net worth. Not portfolio value. Distributions per quarter.

Most W-2 professionals build the wrong asset.

A 401(k) you can't touch until 60. Stock options that vest if you stay. Home equity you can't eat.

All of it tied to one input — earned income from a job that, statistically, you will leave.

Cash flow is the alternative. It's not glamorous. It's not a 10x return story. It just shows up every quarter.

Build it slowly. It will outlast every job you ever take.
