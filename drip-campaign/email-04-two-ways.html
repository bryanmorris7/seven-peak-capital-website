The Two Engines

 

There are only two ways to make money in real estate.

Income. Or appreciation.

That's the whole list. Anything else is just a rebrand of those two.

Most investors are sold on appreciation. The market goes up, the property goes up, you sell, you win. It's the version that fits in a tweet.

It's also the version that requires a buyer, a market, and a window — three things you don't control.

If you have to sell to win,
you're not investing — you're trading.

Income is the other path. Less photogenic. Doesn't post well on LinkedIn.

It's also what compounds without permission from the market.

At Seven Peak Capital, we underwrite for income first. Appreciation is a bonus we don't model into the base case. If a deal only works because rents 10x or cap rates compress, it's not a deal — it's a hope.

That bias shows up everywhere — in our markets, our debt, our holds, our exit assumptions. You'll see it in the next five emails.
