Active vs. Passive

 

Active real estate is a job.

A second one. With worse hours.

Most investors I talk to start the same way: read a book, buy a duplex, learn the hard way that real estate income isn't passive when you're the one fixing the water heater at 11pm.

There's nothing wrong with the duplex. It builds skill. But it doesn't build leverage.

The shift that matters is from operator to allocator.

OPERATOR

Trades hours for dollars

Manages tenants, vendors, repairs

Capped by personal capacity

Income tied to time

ALLOCATOR

Trades capital for cash flow

Hires the operator instead

Scales with diligence, not labor

Income decoupled from time

Most professionals already make above-market income. The mistake is using that income to buy a job.

Use it to buy ownership in things other people run.

Three lessons left. Then a clear ask.
