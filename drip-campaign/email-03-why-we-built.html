The Origin Story

 

Why we built Seven Peak Capital.

It didn't start in a boardroom. It started with a $55,000 salary and a Lowe's credit card.

In 2016, I was two years out of college, working at Pepsi, making $55K a year. Not exactly the launchpad most real estate stories start with.

Then I read Rich Dad Poor Dad.

It clicked immediately: I needed to own real estate assets that paid me — separate from my reliance on a salary and a corporate job.

I needed assets that paid me
whether I showed up to work or not.

2016. The first house.

An $88,000 pre-foreclosure. 20% down. About $20,000 all-in to close — basically every dollar I had.

The rehab materials went on a Lowe's credit card I paid off over 12 months. The labor came from my mom and dad swinging hammers on weekends with me.

No fund. No partners. No mentor. Just a $55K paycheck, two parents who showed up, and a thesis I'd read in a book.

A year later, I refinanced and used the proceeds to buy a 3-unit multifamily. That property started sending me income every month — whether I worked that month or not.

That's when it clicked. Passive income wasn't theory. It was scalable.

From operator to allocator.

Over the next several years, I built a small portfolio, moved into tech sales, and kept stacking. The triplex taught me real estate. It also taught me the limits of doing it solo.

I shifted from buying small rentals to investing as an LP alongside experienced operators on much larger deals. 200-unit communities instead of 3-unit walkups. Same income mechanics. None of the late-night maintenance calls.

Friends and colleagues at Salesforce, Google, and other tech companies started asking the same three questions:

"How do I get into this — without becoming a landlord?"

"Can I invest passively without quitting my job?"

"Where do I even find deals like this?"

2023. Seven Peak Capital.

My co-founder Chris and I formalized what had been informal for years. We built a firm to do one thing well: help high-earning professionals invest passively in private real estate, without learning the trade through painful trial and error.

Four principles run the firm. They're not marketing. They're how we make decisions.

01  ·  INVESTOR-FIRST

We invest our own capital in every deal we share. If we won't put our money in, we won't ask you to.

 

02  ·  RADICAL TRANSPARENCY

You'll always know how your investment is performing — and why. Monthly and quarterly updates. Real numbers. No spin when the market gets bumpy.

 

03  ·  EDUCATION-DRIVEN

Informed investors make better long-term decisions. This 12-email sequence is the proof of concept.

 

04  ·  REAL ALIGNMENT

We win when you do. The structure is built so our compensation depends on you hitting your preferred return first.

That's the why. The next nine emails are the how.
