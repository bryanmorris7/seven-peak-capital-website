What you can do inside.

01

Browse open offerings

See every active deal, the full offering memorandum, projected returns, sponsor materials, and live capital raised. The Income Fund I waitlist for the 2026 vintage lives here.

02

Get verified once

Accreditation runs through Parallel Markets — a third-party verification service. Submit once, valid for 90 days across every deal you look at. No more re-uploading W-2s every quarter.

03

Subscribe and fund

Sign your subscription docs in-portal. Wire instructions are pre-populated. Most investors complete the full process in under 20 minutes.

04

Track distributions

Every distribution lands in the portal — date, amount, source. ACH or wire to the account on file. Year-to-date totals always visible.

05

Pull tax docs at tax time

K-1s, year-end statements, and capital account history — all downloadable. Send your accountant a single PDF, not 14 emails.

06

Read monthly and quarterly updates

Every property gets monthly and quarterly reports. Occupancy, NOI, debt status, market notes. Good news and bad — same format every cycle. No spin.
